# Changelog

## 1.0

- **Smoother phoneme joins in Queue mode.** Clock the GATE input and step through vowels with SOUND CV to hear one vowel glide into the next. Try bank 7 with SPEED low and FX fully left.
- **Revised robot, alarm, and beep sounds** from Andrew Davie’s SpeakJet 0.5.5 engine, with updated tone transitions and levels.
- **All 14 banks keep their existing order**, so your patches still select the same words, phonemes, and effects.

Tested on Talko 2 across all 347 selections, with zero audio underruns in the final hardware run.
